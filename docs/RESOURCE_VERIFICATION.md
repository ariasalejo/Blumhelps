# Resource verification policy

A listing must not be marked verified solely because a contributor submitted it.

## Minimum review
1. Confirm the source belongs to the named organization or an authoritative public body.
2. Confirm the service exists and the URL is reachable.
3. Record geographic coverage, eligibility, cost, and contact details only when confirmed.
4. Record the date checked and review status.
5. Re-check high-impact listings on a defined schedule and when a report is received.
6. Mark listings stale or withdraw them when evidence cannot be reconfirmed.

## Suggested statuses
- `unreviewed`: submitted but not checked.
- `verified`: source and key details checked; last-reviewed date shown.
- `stale`: needs another review.
- `withdrawn`: no longer recommended or source could not be validated.

Do not publish sensitive personal data or user-submitted allegations as facts. The app is not an emergency-response service or a substitute for professional advice.
